> **Important:** We strongly recommend not using this feature to send your MMRs directly to the terminal without first reviewing them. Haldi Tech is not responsible for errors or incomplete information sent to the terminal.

If you choose to auto-send MMRs without reviewing them first, here are the instructions.

1. Go to **Maintenance > MMR Settings** and configure the settings to your preference.
2. Make sure all of your trucks have the correct domicile set (**Domicile for MMR (optional)**), and the **Schedule B** box is checked in the truck profile.
3. Go to **Settings > Linehaul Terminals** and enter the name and email address(es) for each terminal you need to submit MMRs to. These are the **MMR Contact** and **MMR Email** fields. If you want to receive a copy, put your email address in the **Internal MMR Email** field.

If everything is configured correctly, MMRs will auto-send to the terminal on the 19th of each month.
