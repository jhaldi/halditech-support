# How to View Net Revenue After Fuel

Net revenue after fuel is shown directly on each settlement's **Summary** tab — there's no column to set up.

## Step 1. Open FedEx Settlements.

In the left menu, click **Settlements**, then **FedEx Settlements**.

![Left menu with Settlements expanded and FedEx Settlements selected](img-1.png)

## Step 2. Open an individual settlement.

Double-click the settlement you want to review (or select it and click **Edit**). It opens on the **Summary** tab.

![FedEx Settlements list — double-click a settlement to open it](img-2.png)

## Step 3. Read net revenue after fuel.

The **Summary** tab shows two side-by-side breakdowns:

- **FedEx Settlement P&L** — gross revenue (Linehaul + Spot), then fuel and repairs subtracted, down to a **Net Total**.
- **HaldiTech P&L** — total revenue with fuel taken out as Cost of Goods Sold, giving **Gross Profit** (your revenue after fuel), and continuing down to Operating and Net Income.

The **Net Fuel Revenue / Mi** tile at the top shows your net fuel revenue per mile at a glance.

![Summary tab showing the Net Fuel Revenue / Mi tile and the FedEx Settlement and HaldiTech P&L breakdowns](img-3.png)
