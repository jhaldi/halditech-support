# How to Delete a Settlement

To delete a settlement, go to **Settlements**, then click on the row for the settlement you want to delete. A blue **Delete** button will appear at the top of the screen. Click **Delete** and the system will ask you to confirm you want to delete that settlement.

> **Note:** Only one settlement can be deleted at a time.
