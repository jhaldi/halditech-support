# How to Delete a Settlement

To delete a settlement, go to **Settlements > FedEx Settlements** and click the row for the settlement you want to delete to select it. Then click **Delete** on the toolbar — the system will ask you to confirm.

![The FedEx Settlements list — select a settlement, then click Delete on the toolbar](img-1.png)

> **Note:** Only one settlement can be deleted at a time.
