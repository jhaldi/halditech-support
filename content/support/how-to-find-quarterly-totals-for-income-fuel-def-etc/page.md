## Step 1. Pull the quarter's data

Go to **FedEx Settlements > Bulk Settlement Data**, then select **Last Quarter** from the quick-pick dropdown and hit the **Get History** button.

> _[screenshot to be recaptured]_

## Step 2. Read the totals by section

Once the results load, scroll down:

- The **first section** gives you LH miles and revenue totals (scroll all the way to the right).

> _[screenshot to be recaptured]_

- The **third section** gives you fuel gallon and cost totals.

> _[screenshot to be recaptured]_

- The **fourth section** gives you total costs for repairs/DEF.
