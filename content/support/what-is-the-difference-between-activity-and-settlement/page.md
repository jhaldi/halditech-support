---
title: "What is the difference between \"Activity\" and \"Settlement\"?"
slug: "what-is-the-difference-between-activity-and-settlement"
tags: [Settlements, Reporting]
summary: "Explains how the Activity and Settlement date views differ when sorting data around month boundaries."
freshdesk_id: 150000177204
---

There are two ways to look at dates:

- **Activity** — the dates in the system sort based on the date associated with that item on a settlement.
- **Settlement** — the dates in the system sort based on the date of the settlement.

For example, if the 29th and 30th of a month fall on Saturday and Sunday, all activity for those days would show up on the first settlement of the new month. If you choose **Settlement**, the numbers would include the 29th and 30th in the next month.
